# SOURCES

Books and concepts that shape the skills in this repo. This is a reading list, not per-skill attribution. When a skill draws on a new source, add it here.

| Source | Concepts used |
|---|---|
| *Who: The A Method for Hiring*, Geoff Smart and Randy Street | Role scorecard (mission, outcomes, competencies); chronological career audit; asking how a former manager would rate the candidate |
| *Hire With Your Head*, Lou Adler | Outcomes over responsibilities; most significant accomplishment; peeling back a claim to how and why it was done |
| *Just Listen*, Mark Goulston | Lowering defenses before asking for facts; finding what a candidate is moving toward, not only away from |
| Gaucher, Friesen and Kay (2011), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*; Castilla and Rho (2023), *Management Science* | Gender-coded wording in job postings: an effect in lab studies, a very small one at scale, so a light wording check, not a lever |
| Levashina, Hartwell, Morgeson and Campion (2014), "The Structured Employment Interview: Narrative and Quantitative Review of the Research Literature", *Personnel Psychology* | Evidence judged per predefined attribute; probing and note-taking; interviewer behavior as a source of error |
| *Noise*, Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony and Cass Sunstein | Independent judgments before group discussion; one strong impression coloring every axis |
| Koenecke et al. (2020), "Racial disparities in automated speech recognition", *PNAS* | Speech-to-text errors differ by speaker, so check transcript quotes before acting on them |
